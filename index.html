Millets: grains our grandparents trusted

Millets are small, hardy grains that grow with very little water and suit the climate of Tamil Nadu. They are rich in fibre, iron and calcium, and they keep you full for longer than polished rice.

Millet Kitchen is a small guide to cooking with them. Start with a bowl of ragi koozh, then try the step-by-step thinai pongal in the gallery.

The five millets we cook with

  • Ragi (finger millet)
  • Kambu (pearl millet)
  • Thinai (foxtail millet)
  • Samai (little millet)
  • Varagu (kodo millet)
