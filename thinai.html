Thinai pongal, step by step

Thinai, or foxtail millet, has a mild, nutty taste and cooks like rice. It is a good source of fibre, and many families eat it during festivals and fasting days.

Thinai pongal swaps rice for millet in the classic breakfast dish. Soak the grain first, and it cooks soft in about 20 minutes.

Use the slides above to follow each step. Download the PDF card to keep the ingredients handy, or listen to the audio tips while you cook.

Pair it with coconut chutney and sambar for a full meal.
